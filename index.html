About

I have been DJing and producing for ten years, starting at 16 in Ireland and now based in Peckham. I hold an HND in Music Production and release my own original music.

I play across all formats. House, commercial, club classics, disco, RnB and open format for bars and private events, through to tech house and harder underground sounds for club sets. Ten years of reading rooms means I play the crowd in front of me rather than a prepared setlist.

Why book me

I also run Project 32, a touring event series that has delivered eight shows across Ireland with peak attendances of 600. So I understand a night from behind the bar as well as behind the decks: how the room builds, why the last hour matters, and what a venue actually needs from a DJ.

Reliable, easy to work with, and equally comfortable warming a room or closing it. I turn up early, I bring my own gear when it is needed, and I read the brief.

I also build the audience side. My channels reach 60,000 people and I am happy to promote a night I am playing.
